MIDI Splitter

by Nine Lives Audio

MIDI Splitter distributes polyphonic MIDI input across up to 16 MIDI channels, enabling advanced voice management and orchestration workflows in your DAW.

This installer will guide you through installing the VST3 and/or Audio Unit versions of the plugin, and optionally the PDF user guide.

Supported DAWs: Logic Pro, Cubase, and any DAW that supports VST3 or AU plugins on macOS.
